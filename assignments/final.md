# Final

For your final project, you’ll be building on one of your previous prototypes—basically, taking something you already made and making it better.

Your main focus should be on gameplay. That doesn’t mean visuals don’t matter, but focus on how visuals can support and enhance game feel.

Keep it small. Make something tight, polished, and fun. Think quality over quantity.

Start by picking one of your prototypes that feels like it has the most potential, and then imagine ways it can be even better. Games can ALWAYS be better!
